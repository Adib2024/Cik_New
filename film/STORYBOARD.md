# Pagewise: 15 s vertical promo (1080x1920, 30 fps, BM)

## PAGE
- **Problem:** Malaysians send clean IC copies to banks, telcos and agents, and the copy can be reused for anything.
- **Audience:** everyday Malaysian phone users (loan, phone line or rental paperwork).
- **Goal:** open Pagewise and stamp the IC before sending.
- **Emotion:** quiet relief, "my copy can't be misused, and it never left my phone".
- **One true thing:** Palang IC stamps "UNTUK KEGUNAAN … SAHAJA" on the IC photo, entirely on the device.

## Ideas
1. **UI documentary of one task (chosen):** one specimen IC, typed purpose, stamp lands, the lock badge from the logo snaps onto the card, and the card becomes the logo.
2. Held shot: an IC on a desk while notifications try to "upload" it and fail. Too much invented UI.
3. A typographic essay on "salinan" (copy). Too abstract, and the stamp is the brand's own proof.

Chosen because the lock badge on a page *is* the logo. Cover the logo and the film still says "Pagewise": the badge arriving on the stamped card is that idea and no competitor's.

## World
Light lavender ground (#f4f4fc) with indigo light falling from the top (the site hero's glow) and fine grain. The card is a physical object with soft shadow and a slight perspective tilt. Act 3 shifts the light to green (privacy). End on the same light ground.

**Hero moment:** the green lock badge crashes onto the stamped card's corner (S3).
